I uncover stories hidden in data.

I bring scientific data to cinematic life.

I build cinematic scientific visualizations from real research data: galaxies, extreme weather, planetary systems, climate change. About

I connect technical depth with creative direction.

I’m Kalina Borkiewicz, an interdisciplinary visual computing researcher and R&D leader with a career spanning research, production, journalism, and creative technology. I spent a decade at the National Center for Supercomputing Applications, rising from research software engineer to director.

I’m now pursuing a PhD at the University of Utah to deepen my research into visual communication and human-centered technology. It’s a continuation of an established practice: finding new directions, making them tangible, and bringing together the people who can take them further.
